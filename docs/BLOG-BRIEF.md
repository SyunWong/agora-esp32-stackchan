# Blog companion brief

Working title: **Build a Talking Desktop Robot with Agora and ESP32**

Subtitle: **Bring conversational AI and real-time calling to StackChan.**

Audience: developers who saw the Agora × Espressif integration announcement and
want a physical project they can build and customize.

Open with the robot and a short demo. Explain the payoff: the same desktop
companion can answer as an AI or carry the voice of someone calling from a
browser. Then introduce the division of work: ESP32 handles the physical audio
and expression, Agora handles real-time media and conversational AI, and the
small cloud service coordinates sessions.

Follow with the first AI conversation and the human-calling extension. Put
implementation details beside the relevant steps; keep the engineering diary
out of the main narrative. End by inviting readers to customize a persona,
voice or use case and share their own version.

Primary call to action: open the public recipe and complete the first working
conversation. Secondary: build the browser-call extension and share a demo.
Add repository/video URLs only after they exist. Avoid “first,” “production-ready,”
“works on every ESP32,” measured-latency claims or implied official endorsement.
